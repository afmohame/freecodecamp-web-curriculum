When: 15 to 17 February 2026 Where: Binche centrum

History

The Binche Carnival is a traditional festival held in the town of Binche, Belgium. It is one of the most famous and oldest carnivals in Europe, with roots dating back to the Middle Ages. The carnival is renowned for its vibrant costumes, lively parades, and unique traditions, including the iconic Gilles, who wear elaborate costumes and throw oranges to the crowd as a symbol of good luck.

Location

The Binche Carnival takes place in the town of Binche, located in the Walloon region of Belgium. The town is situated about 60 kilometers southwest of Brussels and is easily accessible by train or car. The carnival events are held throughout the town, with parades and festivities taking place in the streets, squares, and public spaces.

Have FUN!!

The Binche Carnival is a lively and colorful event that offers a unique cultural experience for visitors. The carnival features a variety of activities and events, including parades, music performances, traditional dances, and street performances. Visitors can also enjoy local food and drinks, as well as shop for souvenirs and crafts from local vendors. The carnival is a great opportunity to immerse oneself in Belgian culture and traditions while having fun with friends and family.
